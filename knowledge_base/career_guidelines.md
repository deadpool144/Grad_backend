# Career & Resume Guidelines

This guide provides strategies and tips to help students and fresh graduates succeed in their career searches.

## 1. Resume Design & Formatting Rules
- **Formatting**: Keep your resume to exactly one page if you have less than 5 years of experience. Use standard fonts (like Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman) in 10-12pt size.
- **Header**: Include your full name, phone number, email address, LinkedIn profile URL, and GitHub (for developers) at the very top. Do not include your date of birth, photo, or physical address unless explicitly requested.
- **Bullet Points**: Use the Google XYZ formula: "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]". Start each bullet with a strong action verb (e.g., Developed, Optimized, Orchestrated).
- **Sections**: Structure your resume with:
  1. Professional Summary (Optional)
  2. Experience (Reverse Chronological Order)
  3. Projects (Highlight technologies used and impact)
  4. Education (Degree, major, GPA if > 3.5, graduation year)
  5. Skills (Grouped by category, e.g., Languages, Frameworks, Tools)

## 2. Ace the Interview
- **STAR Methodology**: When answering behavioral questions (e.g., "Tell me about a time you solved a conflict"), structure your answer using STAR:
  - **Situation**: Define the context or problem.
  - **Task**: Describe your responsibility.
  - **Action**: Explain the actions you took to address the problem.
  - **Result**: Highlight the final outcomes, quantifying them when possible.
- **Company Research**: Spend at least 30 minutes reading the company's website, blog, and recent news before your interview.
- **Ask Questions**: Always prepare 2-3 thoughtful questions for the interviewer (e.g., "What does success look like in the first 90 days of this role?").

## 3. Effective Networking
- **Informational Interviews**: Reach out to alumni working at companies you admire. Ask for a brief 15-minute virtual coffee chat to ask about their career path, not to ask for a job directly.
- **Engage on LinkedIn**: Share updates about your projects, write about things you learn, and leave insightful comments on posts by industry experts.
- **Follow-up**: Send a personalized thank-you note within 24 hours of any networking call or interview.
