# Alumni Connect Platform Guide

Welcome to the Alumni Connect Platform Guide. This guide contains information on how to navigate and use the platform's key features.

## 1. Connecting with Alumni and Students
To build your professional network, you can connect with other verified alumni or students:
- Navigate to the **Directory** or use the **Search** bar at the top of the interface.
- You can filter by name, graduation year, major, current company, or skills.
- Click **Connect** on a user's card to send a connection request.
- The receiving user can accept or reject the request via their **Connections** dashboard or notifications tab.

## 2. Event Discovery and Participation
Alumni Connect hosts a range of virtual and physical events:
- Go to the **Events** tab to view upcoming guest lectures, networking dinners, workshops, and career fairs.
- You can search events by name, date, or location.
- Click **RSVP** or **Register** to secure your spot.
- Real-time updates and notifications will be sent to registered participants if there are schedule changes.

## 3. Real-Time Chat & Messaging
Once connected, users can message each other directly:
- Click the **Messages** icon or go to the chat panel.
- Select a connection from the list to start or resume a conversation.
- The chat supports text messaging, image attachments, typing indicators, and read receipts.
- You can clear your chat history or block spam/inappropriate accounts.

## 4. Profile Management & Visuals
Keep your profile up-to-date to attract connections and opportunities:
- Click your profile photo to access the edit page.
- You can upload a profile avatar and a cover banner image.
- Edit your skills, education history, current employment, and projects.
- Make sure to write a compelling bio!

## 5. Security & Verification Rules
- All users must verify their email address via a One-Time Password (OTP) sent during registration.
- Alumni accounts require additional school email or administration validation to obtain the verified badge.
- Rate limiting is enforced to prevent abuse. If you exceed limits, you might see a "Too many requests" page.
- To report inappropriate posts or comments, click the triple-dot icon next to the post and click **Report**.
